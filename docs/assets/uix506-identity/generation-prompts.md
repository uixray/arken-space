## Эскиз 01

Use Image 1 as the EDIT TARGET: the owner's already selected Kelzerias emblem for the Russian virtual tabletop brand Arkspeys. Make a strictly non-destructive OPTICAL SMALL-ICON ADAPTATION of this same emblem, not a fresh logo concept. The source is a symmetrical silver horned helmet/mask with a long central spear-like crest, swept blade-wings on both sides, within a circular portal with four pointed axis ornaments.
Create ONE flat monochrome emblem, centered, generous clear margin, transparent background and transparent negative spaces. Use solid warm ivory #e8e4da ink only. Preserve the identity: single long center crest continuing into the nose/chin, two swept upward horns, two slanted eye apertures, one simplified blade-wing shape on each side, and a single heavy circular ring. Reduce the four tiny ring ornaments to integrated points; no detailed gems. Simplify aggressively for eventual 16–32 pixel favicons: bold connected masses, wide negative spaces, no texture, no bevels, no metallic shading, no cyan energy, no shadows, no gradients, no fine strokes, no letters or words, no extra faces, no new unrelated symbols. Prefer fewer broad geometric cuts over ornamental lines. Keep the recognizable original silhouette and bilateral symmetry; do not round it into a cute character. This output is a flat raster shape study for a subsequently hand-authored editable SVG draft, not a production-approved replacement. Show only the single adapted emblem, no sheet, mockup, labels or size annotations. Preserve real alpha.

## Коррекция 02

Image 1 is the edit target: the simplified Kelzerias mark from the previous step. Correct ONE problem: remove ALL residual distressed texture, speckles, metallic shading and hairline seams. Completely clean the entire negative space to transparent alpha, including between the helmet, wings and ring. Make the ink perfectly uniform warm ivory #e8e4da. Keep the same recognizable bilateral horned helmet, long central crest, single circular portal and pointed axis silhouette. For a small favicon, merge adjacent small plates into solid flat masses: each side wing should have only two broad tips, the helmet should have only two large slanted transparent eye openings, and no thin engraved lines. Sharpen edges into clean flat vector-like graphic shapes, with wide deliberate transparent gaps, not ragged traces. No texture at all anywhere, no glow or cyan, no text. One symbol alone on genuinely transparent background. This is a cleanup of the SAME chosen emblem, not a new identity direction.

В обоих результатах image_gen остались мелкая фактура/шум в отрицательном пространстве и лишние тонкие швы. Ни один PNG не выбран как готовый favicon. Оба сохранены в reference/ для происхождения решения; не используются в сравнении малых размеров.

Итоговые черновые SVG в marks/ нарисованы вручную как редактируемая геометрия того же силуэта. Это не автоматическая трассировка и не встроенный PNG. Растры не редактировались локальными скриптами. SVG не являются утверждённым каноном и требуют просмотра владельцем.
